# 趴卧与鼠标互动素材

使用内置 `image_gen` 生成，保留原始透明 alpha；原有动作图集不替换。每张补充图集为 4 × 2 帧：趴卧、眨眼、摆尾两帧、流口水两帧、伸爪两帧。

## grey-cat-pixel

素材：[grey-cat-pixel-actions.png](../../src/pet/assets/grey-cat-pixel-actions.png)

最终提示词：

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production desktop pet animation sprite atlas, a NEW companion action sheet.
Input image is a character identity and art style reference only. Preserve this exact pet and style: slender grey and white cat with very large pink inner ears, yellow-green eyes, pink nose, white muzzle blaze, white chest and paws. Medium: crisp pixel art, same pixel density and dark outline.
Create exactly 8 isolated full-body sprites in a strict 4-column by 2-row equally sized grid on transparent background, landscape canvas. All frames show the SAME pet lying prone facing forward slightly toward the viewer's left, body extending to the right, full tail visible. All bodies stay the same size, position and baseline within their cells, with generous clear transparent padding between cells. No crop of paws/tail/ears.
Frame order left to right, top to bottom:
0: relaxed prone with head up, eyes wide open, front paws resting together.
1: EXACT SAME pose and placement, both eyes closed in a quick blink.
2: same prone awake pose with tail raised visibly from behind body.
3: same prone awake pose with tail swung outward, subtly changed from frame 2.
4: prone sleeping eyes closed, head resting on front paws, tiny cute pale blue drool bead at mouth.
5: same sleeping pose with drool bead slightly longer, still small and cute.
6: prone head up attentive, one front paw lifted and reaching toward the viewer's LEFT; other paw and body resting.
7: same as frame 6, reaching paw extended farther left/up to playfully swat a nearby mouse cursor; keep body prone.
Maintain anatomical consistency, exactly four legs. No cursor drawn. No lettering, numbers, labels, grids, borders, checkerboard, background, accessories, scenery, floor, ground shadow or watermarks. Genuine transparent alpha. Keep close to the reference pet rather than inventing a new design.
```

## 拎起姿态

以下四张独立透明图片同样通过内置 `image_gen` 生成，不修改原图。完整身体悬空、四肢自然垂下；运行时围绕颈部小幅摆动。

### grey-cat-pixel

素材：[grey-cat-pixel-held.png](../../src/pet/assets/grey-cat-pixel-held.png)

最终提示词：

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Production desktop pet sprite with genuine transparent background. Reference image gives exact character identity and crisp pixel art art style. Draw ONE full-body sprite of exactly the same pet, front facing, gently picked up at the back of its neck in a playful cartoon desktop interaction. Head near top center, body hangs vertically below the neck, both forelegs loosely dangling down and separated from the torso, both hind legs dangling relaxed below the belly, small tail hanging loosely to the side. It must clearly look suspended in midair, NOT sitting or standing, with relaxed curious expression, no distress. Neck is the visual pivot. Preserve the reference fur colors, face, ears, eye colors, markings and artistic treatment. Small avatar proportions, readable at 96 pixels, full body in frame with 10% transparent margin. No hand, human, strings, collars, props, ground, shadows, text, borders or environment. Only one pet sprite.
```
